Portfolio and Development Suite

Eight integrated AI tools built around a central Data Orchestrator — covering everything from strategy generation and portfolio prioritization to dependency mapping, blueprint creation, and autonomous development. Each tool is capable on its own; together they form a complete end-to-end operating system for enterprise AI delivery.

Hover over any tool in the diagram below to explore its core capabilities.

Suite Architecture

How the Suite Fits Together

The Data Orchestrator sits at the center of the suite — every data movement, every ingest, and every export flows through it. The surrounding tools each handle a distinct function while remaining connected through this shared data layer.

The Tools

What's in the Suite

Each tool in the suite is purpose-built for a specific phase of enterprise AI delivery — from setting strategy to building and monitoring the work.

Data Orchestrator

The central data bus. Every ingest and every export flows through a single point of control — no tool connects to external systems independently.

Strategy Generator

An AI-guided wizard that researches your industry and produces an approved 18-month AI strategy, distributed downstream automatically on sign-off.

Portfolio Builder

Scores and ranks a full set of AI project candidates against your approved strategy, generates business cases, and exports to your project management platform.

Portfolio Intelligence

Real-time PMO health dashboard with AI-generated findings, cost variance detection, and blast radius analysis powered by DependencyIQ.

DependencyIQ

Graph engine that maps projects, systems, and teams as nodes with typed dependency edges — enabling blast radius analysis for any change or delay.

Blueprint Generator

Conversational AI intake that transforms business requirements into structured blueprints, build specs, and automatically created Epics and Stories.

Developer Agent

Polls your project management platform for assigned work, executes build tasks, writes code, and raises pull requests — all without manual handoff.

Portfolio Monitor

Observability layer that captures every event across the suite, tracks KPIs and latency in real time, and routes alerts to email, Slack, Teams, or PagerDuty.

Learn & Optimize

Collects outcome signals from across the suite, adjusts scoring weights based on real results, and surfaces optimization insights back to Strategy Generator and Portfolio Builder.
