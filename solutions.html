How We Work
With You

Every engagement starts the same way. with a conversation about where you are and what you need. From there, three paths forward. No bloated SOWs, no 12-month roadmaps before you see something real.

Four Paths Forward

Find Your Starting Point

Most clients aren't sure which path is right before we talk. That's expected. These four modalities describe how engagements typically take shape. Often, they evolve from one into another.

01
Build With Us
🏗️

Custom Accelerator Build

You have a specific process, problem, or opportunity and you need a working solution fast. We design and build a custom AI accelerator with you, starting from discovery and ending with a deployed, client-validated product. No guessing, no scope creep, just a clear build cycle with real milestones.

What's included

  • Discovery sessions – to map your process, data, and goals
  • Accelerator design – using our Blueprint Generator, requirements become Epics and Stories automatically
  • Working prototype – in weeks, not months, built with our Developer Agent
  • Review cycles – demo, refine, demo again before anything goes to workspace
  • Client workspaces – in your environment, with your data, on your timeline
  • Built to our standard – SOC 2 Type II-aligned security controls and AI governance practices applied from day one, not bolted on later, see Trust & Compliance

Adopt a Product
🚀

Lab Products & Accelerators

If your problem maps to something already in the Lab, you don't need a custom build. You need a fast path to a working product. The Portfolio & Delivery Suite is the delivery framework we built because we needed it, confirmed by client after client. Together with our pipeline of industry accelerators, it is built for exactly this. You still go through our engagement model: demo, refine, workspace, deploy. You just start further ahead.

What's available

  • AI Portfolio & Delivery Suite – strategy, portfolio, dependency, and autonomous development in one integrated platform
  • Industry accelerators – in active development across Retail, Legal, Supply Chain, Real Estate, and Transportation
  • Client workspace – before any commitment, evaluate with your own data in your own environment
  • Continuous improvement – our teams keep iterating with you after deployment

03
Embed Our Team
🤝

AI-Trained Talent

Sometimes the right move is augmenting your team with people who already know how to work with AI, not just talk about it. We place project managers and technologists who have been trained on our accelerator toolkit and delivery practices. This is a conversation, not a catalog. Every placement is shaped around what your team actually needs.

How it works

  • AI bootcamp trained – every person has completed our accelerator toolkit and best practices
  • PMs and technologists – with real AI delivery experience, not just certifications
  • Full-service model – we handle recruiting, training, onboarding, and vendor management
  • Flexible engagement – the structure is built around your team, your timeline, and your work
  • Same governance bar – every placement works to our SOC 2-aligned security standard and AI best practices by default

04
Resell Our Engine
🏷️

White-Label & Reseller Program

Some partners don't want to build AI products themselves, they want to bring one to their own clients under their own brand. We adapt an existing accelerator into a white-labeled product built for your team and your client base, with a small "Powered by XLeratorAI" credit and nothing else revealing whose engine is underneath. It's the same accelerator-to-POC-to-MVP path we use with every direct client, just pointed at your brand instead of ours.

How it works

  • Start from an accelerator – we adapt an existing accelerator from the suite rather than starting from a blank page
  • POC on our cloud – white-labeled and good enough for your team to show internally and get approval
  • Signed contract to MVP – once you're ready, we build the paid MVP your team and your clients will actually use
  • Your cloud or ours – deployment lands wherever fits the engagement best
  • Powered by XLeratorAI – a small, discreet credit; your brand stays front and center
