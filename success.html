Industry Experience

Manufacturing

Meridian Industrial Group

A team member led a 14-month engagement to modernize the PMO function across three North American plants, consolidating more than 80 active capital projects into a single portfolio view. She worked directly with plant operations leaders to map project interdependencies and surface scheduling conflicts that had been invisible to the central PMO. The engagement produced a dependency registry that reduced unplanned downtime on major initiatives by an estimated 22%. She also facilitated monthly portfolio reviews with the VP of Operations, translating technical project data into executive-ready reporting.

Cascade Precision Manufacturing

One of our senior consultants embedded with the engineering PMO at Cascade to establish a structured gating process for new product introduction projects. He built out a stage-gate framework aligned to their ISO quality standards and trained a team of eight project managers on the new process. The work uncovered chronic resource contention between the R&D and tooling teams, which was resolved through a shared capacity model he designed with department heads. His engagement spanned two product launch cycles and was credited with cutting average time-to-gate-approval by 30%.

Fortis Component Systems

A team member supported Fortis during a multi-plant ERP consolidation that had stalled due to unclear ownership across business units. She stepped in as interim program manager, restructured the governance model, and re-baselined the program schedule against realistic resource capacity. She ran weekly steering committee sessions and maintained an integrated risk log that tracked 40+ open items across six workstreams. The engagement stabilized a program that had been 11 months behind schedule and positioned it for a successful cutover six months later.
