How We Work

The Model That Got Us Here

This is the model that took the AI Portfolio & Delivery Suite from accelerator to active Client Workspaces in six months. The same process applies to every accelerator we build, across every industry.

We don't show up with a finished product and ask clients to buy it. We show up with a working accelerator in our XLeratorAI Workspace. Something real, something they can react to. We build the conversation from there.

That first session almost always produces a list of things to improve. We take that list, make the changes in our Workspace, and come back in days, not weeks. The second demo is a different experience. By then, the client recognizes their own feedback in the product.

When they're ready, we spin up a Client Workspace: a white-labeled, dedicated environment with their branding, their data, their team. No commitment required. Just a structured evaluation with something that's already been shaped around their needs.

When they say go, that's a signed contract. From there we deploy where it fits best: your private cloud, or ours. For some engagements, this same path also becomes a white-labeled product resold to a partner's own client base, the reseller motion that built our reseller channel. Same accelerators, same Data Orchestrator, same lead PM methodology, just a different destination for the finished product.
