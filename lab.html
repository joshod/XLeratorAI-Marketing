How Products Are Born

Our Clients Help Us Build

Every product in the Lab starts as an accelerator and becomes a product through structured collaboration with real clients. That's not a pilot, it's how we build.

The AI Portfolio & Delivery Suite is the delivery framework we built because we needed it, and our clients confirmed they did too. It began as three separate accelerators: an AI Strategy Generator, an AI Portfolio Builder, and a Portfolio Intelligence dashboard. Each one solved a real problem. None of them were products yet.

Through structured client sessions (live demos, rapid iteration, workspace evaluations), those accelerators grew. Client feedback shaped the roadmap. The Blueprint Generator captured what needed to change. The Developer Agent executed the changes fast. New capabilities were added. What started as three tools became an integrated, production-ready suite of nine.

The suite kept growing the same way. CortexIQ joined as the platform's context layer, keeping real environment and architecture facts current so Blueprint Generator and the Developer Agent never build against a guess. ITSMI joined to close the loop on the other side: reporting what actually happened to every ServiceNow ticket that became engineering work, and what didn't.

That same model is what every accelerator in the Lab goes through. Clients aren't evaluating software, they're co-creating it.

Suite Architecture

How the Suite Fits Together

The Data Orchestrator sits at the center of the suite. Every data movement, every ingest, and every export flows through it. The surrounding products each handle a distinct function while remaining connected through this shared data layer.

That same connective layer is what let us adapt Portfolio Intelligence into Stage Gate Intelligence, our first white-labeled reseller product, in weeks instead of months.

Click any product below to see its core capabilities. For the full picture, download the Suite Overview below.

1

Strategy to Portfolio

Plan, prioritize, monitor.

  • AI-guided research produces an approved 18-month strategy grounded in real industry intelligence.
  • Human sign-off required before anything moves downstream.
  • Automatically hands the approved strategy to Portfolio Builder.
  • Scores and ranks project candidates against the approved strategy across alignment, ROI, impact, and risk.
  • Generates structured intake forms and business cases for each candidate.
  • Once approved, pushes epics straight into Jira for Blueprint Generator to build.
  • Real-time PMO health dashboard with AI-generated findings and risk alerts.
  • Cost variance detection across every active project.
  • AI analysis and recommendations available for any project on demand.
2

Portfolio to Code

Approved roadmap work becomes shipped code.

  • Conversational, voice-capable intake refines rough requirements into structured build specs.
  • Checked against CortexIQ's live environment context before anything moves forward.
  • Loads Epics and Stories directly into Jira, ready for the Developer Agent.
  • Polls Jira for assigned work, writes code, and runs tests autonomously.
  • Checks CortexIQ's guardrails at the start of every story.
  • A human reviews and merges the pull request. Nothing ships unattended.
3

Requirements/Tickets to Code

The same build engine, started from a support ticket instead of a project.

🎫 ServiceNow → ⚡ Blueprint Generator → 🤖 AI Developer Agent → 🎫 ServiceNow Blueprint Generator and the AI Developer Agent are the same two apps from Portfolio to Code, above. Here they start from a ServiceNow ticket instead of an approved project, and Data Orchestrator writes the completion status back to ServiceNow automatically when it's done.
  • Reports how ServiceNow tickets are actually managed: which became real engineering work, which didn't, and why.
  • Tracks routing rate, build progress, and ticket class breakdown.
  • Closes the loop: completion status writes back to ServiceNow automatically.
4

Context, Data & Risk

The cross-cutting layer every other group draws from.

  • Holds every external credential and makes every outbound call: Jira, ServiceNow, GitHub, Ardoq, and more.
  • No accelerator connects to an external system on its own, by architecture, not just policy.
  • Every data movement between all 9 apps is mediated through one gateway.

See how we secure this architecture →

  • Pulls real architecture and environment data from Ardoq's enterprise topology and ServiceNow's CMDB.
  • Keeps guardrails and environment facts current and human-verified, not just AI-assumed.
  • Feeds Blueprint Generator, the AI Developer Agent, and DependencyIQ directly.
  • Maps projects, systems, and teams as a dependency graph with typed relationships.
  • Surfaces collisions, bottlenecks, and blast radius before a change causes a real problem.
  • Built on CortexIQ's real topology data, not assumptions.
