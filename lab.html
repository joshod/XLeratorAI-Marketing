How Products Are Born

Our Clients Help Us Build

Every product in the Lab starts as an accelerator and becomes a product through structured collaboration with real clients. That's not a pilot — it's how we build.

Our flagship AI Portfolio & Delivery Suite began as three separate accelerators: an AI Strategy Generator, an AI Portfolio Builder, and a Portfolio Intelligence dashboard. Each one solved a real problem. None of them were products yet.

Through structured client sessions — live demos, rapid iteration, sandbox evaluations — those accelerators grew. Client feedback shaped the roadmap. The Blueprint Generator captured what needed to change. The Developer Agent executed the changes fast. New capabilities were added. What started as three tools became an integrated, production-ready suite of eight.

That same model is what every accelerator in the Lab goes through. Clients aren't evaluating software — they're co-creating it.
